<h2>📈 ABC Company Sales Performance Dashboard</h2>
Welcome to the ABC Company Sales Performance and Analytics project! <br>
This repository features an interactive, enterprise-grade business intelligence dashboard designed to monitor macro-level financial health, analyze retail distribution, and optimize product sales strategies. By synthesizing high-volume transactional data, this dashboard transforms raw revenue metrics into actionable commercial strategies.<br>

<h3>📊 Dashboard Focus Areas</h3>
The dashboard is structured across three core analytical perspectives to drive business decisions:<br>
<strong>1. Sales Overview: </strong>A high-level financial health check tracking critical metrics including $350.5M in Total Sales, 132K Orders, and 329.8K Items Sold across 32 Brands with a clear breakdown of seasonal revenue pacing.<br>
<strong>2. Customer Insights: </strong> A behavioral mapping view analyzing 1,765 unique customers with a structured breakdown of Sales by Customer Satisfaction (categorized by Good, Best, and Better sentiments) to align product quality with buyer sentiment.<br>
<strong>3. Performance Analysis: </strong> A competitive product and brand audit ranking operational outliers, featuring dedicated deep-dives into the Top 5 and Bottom 5 Sold Brands by revenue and volume (e.g., Diabetic, Fashion, Ballet, and Adidas).<br>
