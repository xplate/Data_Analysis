
<h2>🏆 FIFA World Cup Data Analytics Dashboard</h2>

Welcome to the FIFA World Cup Data Analytics project! This repository contains an interactive data dashboard designed to explore, analyze, and visualize historical data from every FIFA World Cup tournament. By transforming raw historical sports metrics into dynamic visual insights, this project uncovers the trends, dominance, and evolution of international football.<br><br>

<strong>📊 Dashboard Overview</strong> <br>
The dashboard provides a high-level macro view of World Cup history, summarizing decades of competitive sports data into actionable visual formats: <br>
Core KPIs: Tracking high-level metrics, matches played, and total goals scored across unique participating nations.<br>
Historical Dominance: Visualizing international tournament winners and team appearances.<br>
Performance Dynamics: Analyzing the balance of Home vs. Away Goals and tracking the evolution of Goals By Year.<br>
Tournament Structure: Breaking down Goals By Stage to see how scoring patterns shift from the Group Stages to the high-stakes Semi-finals.<br>
Hosting Insights: Mapping out global hosting frequencies across different nations over time.<br><br> Enjoy!
